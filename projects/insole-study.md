# Insole design and prototype planning

**PLTW Engineering Design & Development · Senior-year team study**

## Goal

Explore an insole intended to improve comfort during prolonged standing. The project developed material and prototype plans, with sensing considered as a possible extension.

## Planning work

The documentation compares materials such as craft foam, silicone rubber, PVC-backed fabric, and memory foam. The bill of materials also considers valves and adhesive for an air-containing structure.

The team listed equipment for cutting, shaping, and compressing materials, and considered how machining or laser equipment might help produce more consistent geometry. The notes weigh buying raw materials against the added cost of preprocessed parts.

## Sensing exploration

The planning document lists possible load cells, HX711 amplifier modules, a microcontroller, and breadboard wiring. Those entries describe a potential direction. They do not establish a working pressure-mapping insert.

## Outcome and contribution

I participated in the team’s design and prototype planning. The available files document material selection, resource planning, and design research. They contain no sensor data, calibration record, or completed pressure-mapping system.

## Next iteration

Separate comfort prototyping from sensing development. Build and evaluate the material stack first, then test a single sensor with known loads before committing to a wearable layout.

**Sources:** prototype-planning worksheet and senior project proposal. Market and health claims in the coursework are not presented here as verified project results.
